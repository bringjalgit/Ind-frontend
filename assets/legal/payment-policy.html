Payment, Cancellation, Refund & Shipping Policy

IND CLASSIFIEDS · Last updated: 2 July 2026
Welcome to IND CLASSIFIEDS. Our platform facilitates connections between buyers and sellers, allowing them to interact and negotiate transactions independently. Please read the following policy carefully, as it outlines the responsibilities and expectations regarding payments, cancellations, and refunds on our platform.

1. Payment Policy

  • Direct Transactions: All payments for goods and services are made directly between buyers and sellers. IND CLASSIFIEDS does not process or facilitate any transactions, and it is the sole responsibility of the buyers and sellers to agree on payment methods and terms.
  • Payment Methods: Buyers and sellers should mutually agree upon a secure payment method. Options may include cash, bank transfer, or any other method deemed safe and convenient by both parties.
  • Security: We strongly advise all users to use secure payment methods and be cautious of potential scams. Verify the legitimacy of the buyer/seller and item before proceeding with any transaction.

2. Cancellation Policy

  • Negotiated Cancellations: Cancellations are to be negotiated directly between buyers and sellers. Both parties should communicate openly to reach an agreement if either party wishes to cancel a transaction.
  • Conditions for Cancellation: Buyers and sellers may establish their own terms and conditions for cancellation before finalizing any transaction. This may include time frames, restocking fees, or other relevant conditions.

3. Refund Policy

  • No Refunds: We do not offer refunds for any digital services, including ad packages or listing upgrades. All purchases are final and non-refundable, including accidental or duplicate purchases.
  • Failed Payments: If a payment fails or is deducted but the listing/package is not activated, please contact our support team at support@indclassifieds.in with your transaction details, and we will resolve it promptly.
  • Buyer–Seller Transactions: Refunds for items bought or sold between users are managed directly between the buyer and seller. IND CLASSIFIEDS does not handle or mediate such refunds.

4. Shipping Policy

  • Buyer and Seller Responsibility: All shipping responsibilities are to be discussed and agreed upon by the buyer and seller. This includes shipping methods, costs, and timelines.
  • No Liability: IND CLASSIFIEDS holds no responsibility for shipping arrangements or issues. Any disputes or problems related to shipping must be resolved directly between the buyer and seller.

5. Disclaimer

  • No Liability: IND CLASSIFIEDS is not responsible for any disputes or issues arising from transactions between buyers and sellers, including payments, cancellations, and refunds. We do not provide mediation services or guarantee the outcome of any negotiation.
  • User Responsibility: Users are encouraged to perform due diligence and exercise caution when engaging in transactions. Ensure all agreements are clearly documented and understood by both parties.

6. Reporting Issues

Feedback and Reporting: If you encounter any fraudulent activity or wish to report an issue, please contact our support team at support@indclassifieds.in. While we do not intervene in individual transactions, we take reports of misconduct seriously and will take appropriate action against users who violate our terms of service.
