9. Location Data Collection and Usage

We may collect and use your device's location data to enhance your experience on our platform. This applies to both our mobile app and website.

9.1 What Location Data We Collect

  • Precise location data (via GPS, Wi-Fi, or mobile network) when you enable location services on your device.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address if GPS is disabled.

9.2 How We Use Location Data

  • Displaying relevant classified listings (e.g., jobs, products, rentals) near your area.
  • Helping users post ads with accurate location tagging.
  • Personalizing content and recommendations based on your region.
  • Improving search results and service quality in your location.

9.3 User Control

  • Enable or disable location access anytime via your device settings.
  • Choose to enter your location manually when posting or browsing listings.

9.4 Sharing of Location Data

We do not sell or share your precise location with third parties for marketing. Your data may be shared:

  • With service providers (e.g., hosting or analytics) under strict confidentiality.
  • As required by law or legal processes.

9.5 Storage and Security

Location data is stored securely and retained only as long as necessary for the purposes above. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.
